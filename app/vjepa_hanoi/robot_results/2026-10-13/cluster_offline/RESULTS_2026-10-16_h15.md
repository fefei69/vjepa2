# H15 with the one-move setup: offline check over all 90 held-out moves (2026-10-16)

**Question.** Does the setup that works on one move keep working on every move of the six H15 tasks? The H15 result
only makes sense if the per-move success inside H15 is similar to H1's. The H1 setup is: stage subgoals, the best of
51 training actions, the low-lateral guard, the box, 36 mm steps, schedule ×1.5.

**Method.**
- **Replay.** `tools/descent_replay.py --until release`: a closed loop on recorded frames, from the hover above the
  source peg. `--archive data/expert6_single_move/test.npz` holds the 90 held-out moves (episode 9 of the six tasks,
  15 moves each). `--goal_archive data/expert6_single_move/train.npz` holds the goals, the 51 actions and the box,
  unless a row says otherwise.
- **Report.** `tools/h15_replay_report.py` breaks the result down by move number, ring and task.
- **"H1 rule".** The jaw opens within 10 mm (5 mm sideways) of the demo's release pose, the same success rule as the
  one-move results.
- **"Over the rod".** Also counts an opening up to 15 mm higher that is still within 5 mm sideways and below the low
  floor (166 mm). There the ring is around its rod, so whether it slides down onto the peg decides the move on the arm.

## Findings

- **Later moves do as well as first moves** (E-1fps: 45/84 against 3/6), so the per-move skill holds across H15.
- **The 51 actions must come from all moves.** With the first moves' set, later moves go off path (8/84).
- **So must the box.** The first moves' box stops at 85.7 mm, but later moves grasp and place down to 56.5 mm.
- **Most misses in the arm setting are early releases.** In 34 of E-1fps's 42 misses the jaw opens 11–15 mm above the
  demo's release pose, over the rod.
- **Ring 4 (move 8)** is 3/6 in the arm setting. At full steps it is 0/6: the jaw stays open 5 mm above the ring.
- **Training twice as long changes nothing:** A-expert-1fps equals E-1fps (48/90).
- **The play model needs the expert's goals, actions and box.** With them it is 41/90; with its own play goals and
  actions it goes off path under the 36 mm cap (69 of 90 moves). Both expert-goal bundles therefore use the same
  `goals.npz` (sha256 1aff095d…), and the H15 expert and play runs differ only in the world model.
  `robot_bundle_play1fps_xg` is a hard link of `robot_bundle_play1fps/weights.pt` (sha256 2e2f425a…) plus that goals
  file.
